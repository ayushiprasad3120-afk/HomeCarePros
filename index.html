FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services can I request?

You can request help with roofing, HVAC, pest control, plumbing, bathroom remodeling, and windows. All six categories are handled through the same request process.

How does HomeCarePros work?

You tell us what you need by calling or submitting the quote form. We then work to connect you with an independent local professional who serves your area and the category you selected.

Is there a cost to request a quote?

No. Requesting a quote or calling to ask about your project costs nothing.

Will I be connected with a local professional?

We work to connect you with an independent professional who serves your area and category. Availability can vary depending on your location and the service requested.

Which areas do you serve?

Service availability depends on your ZIP code and the professionals who serve that area. Submit a request and we'll let you know what's available near you.

Can I call instead of submitting the form?

Yes. You're welcome to call the number listed on this site at any time instead of filling out the form.
