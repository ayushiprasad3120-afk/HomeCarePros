Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

HomeCarePros ("HomeCarePros," "we," "us," or "our") operates this website to help homeowners get connected with independent local service professionals. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you call the phone number listed on this site or submit a quote request, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and contact information (phone number, email address)
  • Your ZIP code or general service area
  • The service category and details about the project you're inquiring about
  • Information automatically collected through standard web technologies, such as browser type, device type, and pages visited

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and connect you with a service professional
  • Route phone calls and quote requests to an appropriate service professional
  • Improve this website and the services we offer
  • Comply with legal obligations

How We Share Your Information

To connect you with help for your project, we may share the information you submit with independent, third-party service professionals who provide services in your area and category. We do not control how those independent professionals use or store your information once shared, and we encourage you to review their own privacy practices.

We do not sell your personal information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Phone Calls

Calls to the phone number listed on this site may be recorded or monitored for quality assurance and routing purposes, where permitted by law.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to support basic site functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You may decline to provide certain information, though this may limit our ability to connect you with a service professional. To ask a question about your information, use the contact details in our quote section.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Not a Contractor

HomeCarePros is a marketing and lead-generation website and is not a contractor. HomeCarePros does not perform roofing, HVAC, pest control, plumbing, bathroom, or window services. Services are provided by independent service professionals, not by HomeCarePros.
